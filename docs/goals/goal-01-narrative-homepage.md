# Goal 1 — Narrative homepage restructure

**Status:** Implemented for review  
**Created:** 2026-09-28

## Goal

Transform the WisConnect homepage from a Phase 1 information inventory into a focused seven-chapter story:

1. Hero — what WisConnect is
2. Why WisConnect exists
3. People · Capital · Communities — the cooperative model
4. What WisConnect unlocks — member value
5. The women building WisConnect
6. Enterprise in action — grounded business context
7. Join WisConnect — the invitation

## Research principles

- WBDC: organize services around understandable outcomes rather than internal categories.
- digitalundivided: connect support to a founder journey and use proof only when it is real.
- Buy From A Black Woman: lead with a belief and connect business support to community value.
- Black Girl Ventures: make capital, capability and community easy to understand.
- WEConnect International: explain global connection through the opportunity it creates, not geography alone.
- ChiFresh: make cooperative ownership tangible instead of theoretical.
- Mobbin references (Wise, sweetgreen, Mother Design, Zipline, Patreon): strong proposition, editorial mission narrative, human-forward people presentation, proof near story, and a decisive closing CTA.

## Scope decisions

- Keep the current WisConnect visual identity, hero, cooperative stack, Visionaries interaction and enterprise carousel.
- Remove secondary information from the homepage scroll without deleting the underlying work.
- Add /explore as a secondary destination for programs, resources, directories, updates, FAQs, language availability and privacy information.
- Do not publish unverified impact numbers, unapproved business listings or newly received portraits/bios without publication approval.
- Keep Contact and Join as the existing email-draft flows for this frontend goal.
- No backend/auth changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- The homepage reads as one story rather than a requirements checklist.
- Only seven primary chapters remain visible in the main scroll.
- Navigation points to the visible story and the new Explore destination.
- Secondary content remains represented outside the main homepage story.
- No invented impact claims or fake proof is introduced.
- Mobile and desktop layouts preserve the established WisConnect design language.
